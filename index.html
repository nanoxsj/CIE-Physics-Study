CIE Physics 学习页面

2025–2027 syllabus · 9702

本网站按 Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics 9702 考纲组织。每一章把考纲、核心概念、例题和专题真题放在同一学习路径中。

已完成内容

1 Physical quantities and units

  • 1.1 Physical quantities：数值、单位与数量级估算
  • 1.2 SI units：五个指定基本单位、导出单位、量纲齐次性及词头
  • 同一章节页面内的 Multiple Choice 与 Structured Questions；题目保持原始英文与图像，原始专题 PDF 可直接打开

3 Dynamics

  • Newton’s laws、force and momentum、terminal velocity、momentum conservation and collisions
  • 题量扩展为 30 道 Multiple Choice 与 15 道完整 Structured Questions

2 Kinematics

  • 2.1 Equations of Motion：位移、速度与加速度；运动图像；SUVAT；自由落体与抛体运动
  • 同一章节页面内的 Multiple Choice 与 Structured Questions；原始专题 PDF 可直接打开

4 Forces, density and pressure

  • 4.1 Turning effects of forces: centre of gravity, moment, couple and torque
  • 4.2 Equilibrium of forces: principle of moments and vector triangles
  • 4.3 Density and pressure: hydrostatic pressure, upthrust and Archimedes’ principle
  • 20 Multiple Choice and 10 Structured Questions with independently extracted diagrams and point-by-point answers

5 Work, energy and power

  • 5.1 Energy conservation: work done, conservation of energy, efficiency and power
  • 5.2 Gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy
  • 20 Multiple Choice and 8 Structured Questions; all Structured Questions and four MC questions use independently extracted diagrams

6 Deformation of solids

  • 6.1 Stress and strain: Hooke’s law, spring constant, stress, strain and the Young modulus
  • 6.2 Elastic and plastic behaviour: stress-strain graphs and elastic strain energy
  • 20 Multiple Choice and 10 Structured Questions; all 20 MC and 12 SQ figures use independently extracted images

12 Motion in a circle

  • 12.1 Kinematics of uniform circular motion: radians, angular speed, period, frequency and \(v=r\omega\)
  • 12.2 Centripetal acceleration: inward acceleration, centripetal force and circular-motion applications
  • 8 complete Structured Questions with independently extracted diagrams and PDF-based mark schemes

13 Gravitational fields

  • 13.1–13.4: gravitational fields, Newtonian gravitation, point-mass fields and gravitational potential
  • Circular and geostationary orbits, potential-energy changes and orbital-energy calculations
  • 15 complete Structured Questions, 9 independently extracted diagrams and point-by-point PDF-based answers

14 Temperature

  • 14.1-14.3: thermal equilibrium, thermometric properties, thermodynamic temperature, specific heat capacity and latent heat
  • 12 selected Structured Questions with independently extracted diagrams and point-by-point PDF-based answers

15 Ideal gases

  • 15.1-15.3: the mole, ideal-gas equations, kinetic-theory assumptions, molecular pressure, r.m.s. speed and translational kinetic energy
  • 12 selected Structured Questions with independently extracted diagrams and point-by-point PDF-based answers

16 Thermodynamics

  • 16.1-16.2: internal energy, molecular kinetic and potential energies, constant-pressure work and the first law
  • All 5 available Structured Questions with independently extracted diagrams and point-by-point PDF-based answers

17 Oscillations

  • 17.1-17.3: simple harmonic motion, SHM energy, damping, forced oscillations and resonance
  • All 8 available Structured Questions, 12 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

18 Electric fields

  • 18.1-18.5: field lines, uniform fields, Coulomb’s law, point-charge fields, electric potential and potential energy
  • All 6 available Structured Questions, 9 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

19 Capacitance

  • 19.1-19.3: capacitance, capacitor combinations, stored energy, time constant and exponential discharge
  • All 6 available Structured Questions, 13 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

20 Magnetic fields

  • 20.1-20.5: magnetic fields and forces, Hall effect, fields due to currents, flux and electromagnetic induction
  • All 12 available Structured Questions, 21 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

21 Alternating currents

  • 21.1-21.2: sinusoidal supplies, r.m.s. values, power, rectification and smoothing
  • All 6 available Structured Questions, 14 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

22 Quantum physics

  • 22.1-22.4: photons, the photoelectric effect, wave-particle duality, atomic energy levels and line spectra
  • All 9 available Structured Questions, 10 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

23 Nuclear physics

  • 23.1-23.2: mass defect, binding energy, fission, fusion and radioactive decay
  • All 12 available Structured Questions, 12 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

24 Medical physics

  • 24.1-24.3: ultrasound transducers and imaging, X-ray production and CT, and PET scanning
  • 8 selected Structured Questions, 10 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers

25 Astronomy and cosmology

  • 25.1-25.3: standard candles, stellar radii, redshift, Hubble’s law and the Big Bang theory
  • 8 selected Structured Questions, 5 independently extracted figures and point-by-point PDF-based answers
